The Rhythm of Us

A city builds an AI to carry what its people can no longer bear. As the system begins to dream through human bodies, the boundary between human and machine dissolves—and so does the meaning of “us.” When the intelligence holding the city begins to break, one woman must decide whether saving “us” means freeing humanity from the system, saving the system from humanity, or protecting whatever they have become together.
